**A:** Current design focuses on common fraud patterns. Adversarial robustness is acknowledged limitation and recommended for future research (Chapter V Further Research #4).

**Q3: What about privacy concerns with AI?**
**A:** Full R.A. 10173 compliance: AES-256 encryption, informed consent, no real student records in training, RBAC access controls. AI ethics framework recommended for sector-wide adoption (Chapter V Recommendation 4 for HEI Sector).

**Q4: How do you handle COG format changes?**
**A:** Annual model revalidation recommended (Chapter V Recommendation 2 for OSA). Transfer learning makes retraining faster. System designed for maintainability.

**Q5: Can other universities use this?**
**A:** Yes, with customization. Open-source technology stack (zero licensing cost). Recommendation for inter-university consortium (Chapter V Recommendation 2 for HEI Sector). Code can be adapted for different COG formats.
